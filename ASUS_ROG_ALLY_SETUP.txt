Yamaha PSS-790 MIDI Player & Remapper - Asus ROG Ally & Windows Setup
========================================================================

Thank you for downloading the PSS-790 MIDI Player & Recording Studio!
This application is fully optimized for the Asus ROG Ally Z1 Extreme
(7-inch 120Hz touchscreen, 1080p display at 150% Windows scaling).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUICK-START: 3 WAYS TO LAUNCH
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Option 1: 1-Click Launcher (Recommended)
  Double-tap "Launch-PSS790.bat".
  This automatically removes Windows browser download flags and starts
  the application immediately with zero warnings!

Option 2: Direct Executable (.exe)
  1. Double-tap "PSS790Player.exe".
  2. If Windows SmartScreen appears ("Windows protected your PC"):
     - Tap "More info"
     - Tap "Run anyway"
  You only need to do this once per version!

Option 3: Permanent Manual Unblock
  1. Press-and-hold (or right-click) "PSS790Player.exe".
  2. Tap "Properties".
  3. At the bottom, check the "Unblock" checkbox.
  4. Tap "OK".

*Pro Tip for Handheld Users*:
  If you copy PSS790Player.exe to your Asus ROG Ally using a Micro-SD
  card, USB-C flash drive, or local network share (SMB), Windows will NOT
  attach download flags, and SmartScreen will NEVER appear!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
TOUCHSCREEN FEATURES FOR ASUS ROG ALLY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Fixed Viewport: The app locks cleanly to the 720p screen without
  awkward window scrolling or cropped headers.
- Multi-Select: Tap checkboxes on song rows or tap the [x] Select button
  in the sidebar to select multiple songs.
- Docked Batch Bar: When songs are selected, [Move] and [Delete] buttons
  remain docked at the bottom of the sidebar at all times.
- 120Hz Touch Scrolling: Smooth, native kinetic scrolling on the Ally's
  touchscreen.
- 22px Slider Thumbs: Volume and tempo slider handles are enlarged for
  easy finger dragging.
