Yamaha PSS-790 MIDI Player & Remapper - Asus ROG Ally & Windows Setup
========================================================================

Thank you for downloading the PSS-790 MIDI Player & Recording Studio!
This application is fully optimized for the Asus ROG Ally Z1 Extreme
(7-inch 120Hz touchscreen, 1080p display at 150% Windows scaling).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUICK-START: 3 WAYS TO LAUNCH
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Option 1: 1-Click Launcher (Recommended)
  Double-tap "Launch-PSS790.bat".
  This automatically removes Windows browser download flags and starts
  the application immediately with zero warnings!

Option 2: Direct Executable (.exe)
  1. Double-tap "PSS790Player.exe".
  2. If Windows SmartScreen appears ("Windows protected your PC"):
     - Tap "More info"
     - Tap "Run anyway"
  You only need to do this once per version!

Option 3: Permanent Manual Unblock
  1. Press-and-hold (or right-click) "PSS790Player.exe".
  2. Tap "Properties".
  3. At the bottom, check the "Unblock" checkbox.
  4. Tap "OK".

*Pro Tip for Handheld Users*:
  If you copy PSS790Player.exe to your Asus ROG Ally using a Micro-SD
  card, USB-C flash drive, or local network share (SMB), Windows will NOT
  attach download flags, and SmartScreen will NEVER appear!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
TOUCHSCREEN & HANDHELD NAVIGATION (v2.5.3)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Yamaha PortaSound Studio is built for high-refresh handhelds like the
Asus ROG Ally Z1 Extreme (7-inch 120Hz display):

- Clean Song Library: Instant tap to select, double-tap to play cleanly
  from 00:00 without intrusive popups or tooltips blocking your view.
- Handheld Touch Mode: Tap the 🖐️ Touch button in the header to enlarge
  slider handles, buttons, and song rows to 42px+ for effortless thumb use.
- Continuous Auto-Advance: When a track finishes, the player automatically
  starts the next song in the folder (tap [Auto-Next] to toggle).
- Fixed Viewport: The app locks cleanly to 720p/1080p without window scrolling.
- Multi-Select: Tap checkboxes in select mode for batch moving and management.
- Docked Batch Bar: [Move] and [Delete] remain docked at bottom of sidebar.
- 120Hz Kinetic Scrolling: Native buttery-smooth touch flicking and scrolling.
- Keyboard / Desktop Hotkeys: Full support for Space (Play/Pause), Esc (Stop),
  Up/Down (Navigate list), Enter (Play), L (Loop), and [ / ] (Transpose).

Enjoy making music with your Yamaha PortaSound PSS-790 and PSS-680!
